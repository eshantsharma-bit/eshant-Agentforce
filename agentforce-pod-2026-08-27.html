🔎 This Is Not a Future Risk — It Is Happening Right Now
We queried ~39,000 Revenue Cloud support cases (Jun 2025 – Aug 2026). We found 15 cases where customers were already exploring or blocked on Agentforce functionality in Revenue Cloud — 14 original (Jun 2025–Jul 2026) + 1 new case confirmed in Aug 2026 — with no P&T topic, no trained owner, and no feedback path to the product team.
STEP 1 — Customer hits a gap
Customer is using Agentforce actions in Revenue Cloud — Quoting Agent, PCM actions, product lookups. Something breaks or doesn't work as expected.
STEP 2 — They log a support case
The case enters the standard Revenue Cloud queue. No Agentforce-specific routing. No keyword flag. No alert.
STEP 3 — Lands with a random engineer
Engineer has no Agentforce readiness training. No documented P&T category exists for Agentforce. Case is handled as generic Revenue Cloud.
STEP 4 — Gap never reaches Product
No VOC tag. No P&T report. The product team never learns that customers are blocked. The same gap surfaces again in the next case.

🎯 The Proposal — 3 Components
01
Create a Dedicated P&T Topic for Agentforce
Establish Agentforce as a standalone Product & Topic (P&T) category across Industries and Revenue Cloud.

Assign a named product sponsor per cloud to receive VOC reports and functionality gap summaries.

Today there is no structured path from a support case to the product team for Agentforce issues — this creates one.
02
Automated Case Routing to a Readiness Pod
Build an automated trigger: any case with Agentforce-related signals (product topic, subject keywords, action names) routes immediately to the pod.

Automation alerts pod members at point of case creation — no manual triage.

Pod covers all GEOs — start with 3–5 trained engineers globally. SPOC enforced by design.
03
Structured VOC Feedback Loop — Case → VOC → P&T
Every Agentforce case tagged against a VOC category at close.

Pod lead produces a monthly summary: top blockers, case count, affected accounts — direct to product sponsor.

Sync cadence starts monthly; increases to weekly as volume grows.
